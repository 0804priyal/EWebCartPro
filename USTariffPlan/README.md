# EWebCartPro US Tariff Plan Module

## Overview

The **USTariffPlan** module for EWebCartPro automatically calculates and applies US government tariff charges on international purchases. It ensures compliance with international trade regulations by adding appropriate tariff fees when US customers purchase products from other countries.

## Features

### Core Functionality
- **Automated Tariff Calculation**: Automatically applies tariff charges based on product origin country
- **Multi-Country Support**: Handles purchases from India, UK, Canada, and other countries
- **Seamless Integration**: Tariff charges are automatically included in cart subtotals

### Display & Visibility
- **Frontend Display**: Shows tariff charges in shopping cart and checkout
- **Backend Management**: Complete admin interface for tariff management
- **Order Integration**: Displays tariff charges in order summaries and invoices
- **Transparent Pricing**: Customers see tariff breakdown before completing purchase

### Administration
- **Flexible Configuration**: Manage tariff rates and rules from admin panel
- **Multi-purpose Use**: Can be adapted for other additional charges (shipping, fees, etc.)
- **Country-specific Rules**: Set different tariff rates for different origin countries
